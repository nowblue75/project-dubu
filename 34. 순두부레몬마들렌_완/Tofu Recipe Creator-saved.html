Soft Tofu Lemon Madeleines: A Refreshing Treat

Are you looking for a thoughtful gift for yourself or someone special? These Soft Tofu Lemon Madeleines are the perfect choice. Featuring an unbelievably moist base and a refreshing lemon curd filling, these treats offer a sophisticated experience that captures the essence of spring.

🍋 Ingredients

Item Quantity
Soft Tofu55g (Squeezed)
Cake Flour95g
Unsalted Butter70g (Melted)
Eggs100g (~2 eggs)
Sugar65g
Baking Powder4g
Milk15g
Lemon Juice8g
Lemon Zest1 Lemon

Dietary Preference

Vegetarian✅
Low Butter✅
Refined SugarModerate

Style

Modern Fusion: Classic French pastry technique met with East Asian healthy ingredients.

Cooking Instructions

01

Make the Zesty Lemon Curd

In a saucepan, combine egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, and lemon juice. Mix until smooth, then cook over low heat, stirring constantly. Once thickened, remove from heat and stir in butter using residual heat. Transfer to a piping bag and chill.

02

Batter and Resting

Mix eggs and sugar, then add sifted dry ingredients. Fold in mashed soft tofu and liquids (milk, lemon juice, zest). Finally, incorporate warm melted butter. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight to let the flavors meld.

03

Panning and Baking

Fill a madeleine pan to 80% capacity. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 13-15 minutes. The thermal shock creates that iconic domed "belly."

04

Filling and Decoration

Once cooled, pipe cold lemon curd into the center base. Dip the top half into white coating chocolate and finish with a decorative chocolate flower before it sets.

🎁 Dessert Summary

These Soft Tofu Lemon Madeleines are a delightful fusion of health-conscious baking and gourmet flavors. The soft tofu provides a moistness that traditional recipes often achieve with high butter content, making it an excellent option for those looking for a lighter, "diet-friendly" dessert.
